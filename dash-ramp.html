Dash Ramp

Info

Usefulness: M

Dash Ramp is a good technique for quickly closing the distance and damaging your opponent in more unique way. You can perform this with either block cancel or without it. I highly recommend using the block cancel to lower the chances of you getting traded. Additionally they are good for chaining combos, keeping the momentum and most importantly chasing.

Input

Standard: W + E + SPACE + M1

With block canceling: W + E + SPACE + M1 + M2

Use cases

• Chaining combos and maintaining momentum during aggressive plays.
• Effective chasing tool to hunt down retreating opponents.
• After stunning someone, doing 1 Dash Strike and later connecting it with Dash Ramp creates a very high-value combo.

Tips

• You should watch out for parries when doing dash ramps because getting parried mid air might not always allow you to stun evade.

• Off-region server mechanic: When fighting on high latency, if you do a dash ramp and get stunned mid air, you will be able to stun evade immediately after landing.
