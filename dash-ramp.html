Dash Ramp

Info

Difficulty: Medium

Dash Ramp is a good technique for quickly closing the distance and damaging your opponent in a more unique way. You can perform this with either block cancel or without it. I highly recommend using the block cancel to lower the chances of you getting traded. Additionally, they are good for chaining combos, keeping the momentum and, most importantly, chasing. After stunning someone, doing 1 Dash Strike and later connecting it with a Dash Ramp is a very good combo. It might be hard at the start, but if you get consistent with it, you will improve a lot. To do that, simply press W+E and at the end of the dash press space and M1.

Input

• Standard: W + E + SPACE + M1

• With Block Canceling: W + E + SPACE + M1 + M2

Tips

• You should watch out for parries when doing dash ramps because getting parried mid-air might not always allow you to stun evade.

• For example, when fighting on an off-region server, if you do a dash ramp and get stunned mid-air, you will be able to stun evade after landing.
