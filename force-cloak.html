Force Cloak

Info

Force Cloak allows its user to be almost fully invisible at a small force cost. This ability should be mainly used for hiding, ambushing, and assassinating, which makes it a great tool for Jedi Shadows.

The biggest downside of it is the visibility with movement techniques and not being able to cloak while combat tagged (which has a cooldown of 30 seconds after being hit). It takes around 0.3 seconds to be almost fully cloaked after the input. Once cloaked or after changing your visibility level, it will take around 2.5 seconds to be fully invisible again. Also, you should know that igniting your weapon will disable the cloak.

Cloak Duration

Force Cloak consumes force, so you cannot stay invisible forever. Below is how long the ability lasts based on rank:

  • Advanced Jedi Knight: ~49 seconds
  • Jedi Master: ~57 seconds

Movement Visibility Grades

Movement Type Visibility Level Grade
Standing Still Fully invisible 5
Walking Fully invisible ?You sometimes might become visible if walking on stairs, slopes, or bumpy terrain. 4 - 5
Running Somewhat visible 3
Dashing Almost fully visible ?No one can see your name tag, but the Dashing smoke animation remains fully visible. 2
Skidding Almost fully visible ?No one can see your name tag, but the Skidding smoke animation remains fully visible. 2
Low Flip Almost fully visible ?No one can see your name tag, but if you add a jump at the end, you will be visible. 1 - 2
Flip Almost fully visible ?No one can see your name tag unless you hold any of the movement inputs for the entire duration of the flip. 1 - 2

Tips

• When cloaked, most of the time you should just walk. Using other movement abilities is extremely risky.
